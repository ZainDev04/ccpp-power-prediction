From raw spreadsheet to a model you can trust.

Six years of hourly data from a real plant, UCI Machine Learning Repository.

  1. Audit the raw data

    9,568 hours. Found 41 duplicate rows, 55 humidity readings above 100%, and 182 hours where the vacuum reads above air pressure, so its unit label cannot be right.

  2. Clean with fixed rules

    Duplicates dropped, humidity capped at 100%. Unusual weather kept, because it is real and it is where predictions matter. 9,527 hours remain.

  3. Lock away a test set

    7,621 hours to train, 1,906 sealed for one final test, with the same mix of low and high output in both.

  4. Tune three models

    Support vector regression, k-nearest neighbours and a neural network, each tuned by 5-fold cross-validation on training hours only.

  5. Test once and choose

    KNN was the most accurate and made the fewest costly over-predictions, then got two safety checks for unusual conditions.

Results on 1,906 hours no model had seen.

All three models beat the best published result on this dataset (R² 0.912). The published study used a different split, so the comparison is indicative.
